    5. Context Season + web

    The rules on the wall

    1. 2026 only. One season, no switcher.
    2. Top 10 finishers for the grand prix and for the sprint.
    3. The stamp is the database write time, in UTC. That is the proof the latest refresh ran.
    4. Points on the board are the stored totals. The +N line is this round’s grand prix plus sprint.
    5. The board is public. Anyone can sign in with Google, but chat needs approval while this is a proof of concept: a request emails the site owner, who approves or denies it in an admin panel. Approved emails are saved in a list.
    6. Claude answers from the stored season first. If the board does not have a fact, it should say so instead of guessing.
    7. Recaps are written by Claude from the stored results only: one for each qualifying and one for each race, sprints included. Practice is not covered.
    8. Answers can be wrong. Check the stamp for how fresh the standings are.
    9. Pit Wall remembers what you tell it, such as your name and favorite driver, tied to your Google sign-in. It does not share one person's memory with another.
